How KRISTIVA intends to present products, accounts, purchases and seller responsibilities.

About the platform

KRISTIVA operates a brand site and plans separate region-specific store experiences. Product listings and features become available only after the relevant store and backend have been configured and verified.

Accounts and customer information

Customers must provide accurate information and use their own account. Access permissions and account eligibility will be enforced by the live application. Current preview pages do not create accounts or accept orders.

Products, prices and stock

Only verified product information, prices, shipping terms, seller identity and available stock should be shown before purchase. An item appearing in an illustration is not an offer for sale.

Orders and payments

An order is not confirmed solely because a payment reference or transaction hash has been submitted. Applicable checkout terms, payment verification and product availability are checked before processing.

Installment applications

Any installment journey is conditional on eligibility, regulatory clearance, independent review, a written agreement and the approved store configuration. An application or marketing illustration does not guarantee approval or activate financing.

Returns, disputes and support

Any returns, refunds or disputes will be handled according to the verified product terms, applicable law and a published return procedure. Customers can contact support@kristiva.com for assistance.

Changes and final publication

This is a pre-launch draft for owner and legal review, not a final agreement governing live transactions. A verified business identity, jurisdiction, effective date, contact details and version must be approved before activation.
