KRISTIVA · PRE-LAUNCH POLICY REVIEW

Returns & Refunds

What a fair, evidence-based product issue and refund review should look like.

Draft for review · Not yet effective. This page describes intended processes. No live checkout or financial service is activated through this preview. Final terms require business, legal and operational verification.

Before placing an order

Customers should be able to see the seller, product condition, included items, warranty terms, shipping information and the product-specific return rules before payment.

Problems that can be reported

A customer may report non-delivery, an incorrect item, a damaged package, a defective product or a billing discrepancy. Eligibility and available remedies depend on verified facts, product terms and applicable consumer law.

How to raise a case

Use the customer order-support channel or write to support@kristiva.com with the order reference and a description of the issue. Sensitive information and private one-time delivery codes should not be posted publicly.

Review and decision

The support and fulfillment teams should review delivery status, seller records and evidence before deciding whether replacement, repair, return or refund is appropriate.

Refund processing

A refund is not approved automatically by opening a request. Approved amounts and payment methods must follow verified transaction history and legal rules. No unverified refund period or guaranteed processing time is stated here.

Pre-launch status

Return windows, excluded categories, regional cancellation rights, dispute escalation and service-level timelines need owner and legal sign-off before transactions begin.
