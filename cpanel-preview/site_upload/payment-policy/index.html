KRISTIVA · PRE-LAUNCH POLICY REVIEW

Payment Policy

Clear differences between KRISTIVA's intended regional payment processes.

Draft for review · Not yet effective. This page describes intended processes. No live checkout or financial service is activated through this preview. Final terms require business, legal and operational verification.

International marketplace

The international store is designed for cryptocurrency-based payment through verified checkout instructions. A payment request must display the supported asset, network, receiving details and amount only after configuration.

Independent verification

Submitting a transaction identifier is a review request, not proof of completed payment. The authorized payment system must check network, asset, recipient, amount, confirmations and duplicate use before any order is marked paid. Screenshots do not establish payment.

Bangladesh marketplace

Bangladesh checkout is planned as a separate bKash flow, subject to confirmed merchant credentials and integration testing. bKash is not displayed as a checkout option within the international store.

Payment changes and exceptions

Expired, delayed, incorrect, duplicate, underpaid or overpaid requests need explicit support review and an auditable resolution. Public marketing never publishes wallet addresses or payment instructions.

Conditional installment information

Illustrations of installment plans are informational. Eligibility, approved terms, agreements and any down-payment requirements must be reviewed separately. No percentage, term, country or approval guarantee is promised in this draft.

Operational activation

The live checkout and EMI systems remain subject to technical, legal and business authorization. The preview does not accept payments. Contact payments@kristiva.com about payment-review questions when the service is operational.
