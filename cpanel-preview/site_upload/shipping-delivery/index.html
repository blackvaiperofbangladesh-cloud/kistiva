Shipping & Delivery

The intended purchase-to-delivery process and customer confirmation controls.

Draft for review · Not yet effective. This page describes intended processes. No live checkout or financial service is activated through this preview. Final terms require business, legal and operational verification.

Availability and delivery regions

Items must have real stock, location eligibility and supported shipping details before an order can be accepted. Not every product can be shipped to every region.

Processing and dispatch

An order enters fulfillment after required payment or application approvals and inventory checks. Dispatch, courier assignment and tracking should be shown only when confirmed.

Shipping charges and estimates

Any delivery fees, taxes and estimated arrival times must come from approved operational rules and be shown before checkout. No unverified free shipping or guaranteed delivery time is promised.

Delivery receipt process

The intended system includes a unique, single-use code for each eligible package. A customer uses the matching code inside their own order after receiving the package, and only then can a verified purchase review be considered.

Problems with delivery

For missing, delayed, incomplete or damaged packages, the customer should contact orders@kristiva.com or support@kristiva.com with the order reference. The team should investigate courier and inventory evidence.

Before launch

Shipping destinations, courier agreements, delivery codes, charges, insurance, timelines and return logistics need validation prior to activating fulfillment.
